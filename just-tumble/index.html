[00]Our approach

A crowded mat teaches kids to wait in line. A small group teaches them to tumble.

Just Tumble didn’t start in a building. It started on football fields, teaching athletes how their bodies actually move before anyone asked them to flip.

That’s still the whole idea. When a kid understands why a skill works, the skill sticks — and the confidence comes with it. That only happens when a coach is watching your child, not a line of twelve.

So we keep groups small. Our student-to-instructor ratios are among the lowest in Connecticut and the nation, and every progression is built around the athlete in front of us: where they are today, and the next skill they’re ready for.

Today we’re two 10,000 sq ft gyms, more than 45 people, and the place high school cheer teams and dance studios send athletes when they need real tumbling. The measure hasn’t changed since the football fields: did your child leave with a new skill, and a smile?

Jason Founder, Just TumbleShelton since 2012 · North Haven since 2024

[04]The skill path

Five skills. One order. No shortcuts.

Each skill is built on the one before it. We don’t move an athlete up until the last one is clean — that’s how kids stay safe and keep progressing.

  1. 01

    Cartwheel

    Straight arms, straight legs, a line you could draw with a ruler. Everything starts here.

  2. 02

    Walkover

    Flexibility and control through the bridge — the first skill where the athlete goes upside down on purpose.

  3. 03

    Back handspring

    The milestone. Drilled on the tumbl trak and spotted until it’s powerful, safe and repeatable.

  4. 04

    Back tuck

    Power from the floor, a tight tuck, eyes on the landing. Earned once the handspring is consistent.

  5. 05

    Aerial

    A cartwheel with no hands. Speed, height and a lot of confidence — the whole path in one skill.

[07]FAQ

Parents ask.

The eight questions our front desk hears most. Anything else, call us at 203-654-0031.

Book a free trial
What should my child wear?
  • Hair pulled up securely — no hard clips or headbands
  • No jewelry (small stud earrings are fine)
  • T-shirt or tank top and shorts — no bare midriffs
  • Grippy socks or clean cheer shoes; no bare feet or street shoes
  • A water bottle labeled with their name. Cubbies are provided; leave valuables at home.
Can I stay and watch?

Yes. You’re welcome to stay and watch. Once your child is comfortable, you’re also welcome to leave — just make sure your contact information is current in your account.

How long do we have to commit?

Classes are rolling and run all year. You can cancel any time with 30 days’ notice to the first of the month. If your schedule changes, we’ll help you find a new class.

Is there a sibling or multi-class discount?

Yes — 20% off additional classes, or 20% off for siblings. Discounts don’t apply to the yearly registration fee.

What are your class hours?

North Haven: Mon 9:30–11 AM playgroup, 4:30–8:30 PM classes · Tue 9:30–11 AM playgroup, 4:30–8:15 PM classes · Wed 9:30 AM–12 PM playgroup, 4:30–8:30 PM classes · Thu 4:30–8:15 PM · Sat 8 AM–1:15 PM · Sun 9 AM–12 PM.

Shelton: see the live class schedule in the customer portal, or call 203-654-0031. Private lesson times vary by instructor.

Can my child join a team?

We don’t run competitive teams. Instead, we train athletes from many youth, high school and college cheer teams, and run group classes for dance studios.

What if my child misses a class or gets hurt?

We don’t offer full refunds, but we do offer make-up classes based on availability. If your child is injured, you can pause the membership until a licensed physician clears them to return.

How long has Just Tumble been around?

Since 2012, when we opened in Shelton. Our North Haven gym opened on January 1, 2024.
